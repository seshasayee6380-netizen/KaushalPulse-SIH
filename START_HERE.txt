   Student / Employer / Training Provider / District Admin.

NATIONAL LOCATION MODEL
- Every workspace/page shows State + District market selectors.
- The location list is loaded from a national district reference and cached locally.
- Selecting a new district provisions it in the local database on demand.
- Read-only pages follow the selected market.
- Writes remain protected by the signed-in account's assigned district tenant.

NATIONAL DATA NOTE
- On startup the backend attempts to refresh the district master from the configured national reference source when the local master is incomplete.
- The bundled database is an offline fallback. Do not describe it as a complete current government district list unless a refresh has succeeded.
- The application shows the current local coverage count and evidence source.
